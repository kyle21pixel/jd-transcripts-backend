Frequently Asked Questions
How quickly can I get my transcription?
Our standard turnaround time is 24-48 hours. We also offer expedited services for urgent requests with delivery in as little as 6 hours.
What file formats do you accept?
We accept most audio and video formats including MP3, WAV, MP4, MOV, WMV, and many more. If you have a specific format not listed, please contact us.
How accurate are your transcriptions?
Our professional transcribers maintain a minimum accuracy rate of 99%. For specialized content, we assign transcribers with relevant industry knowledge.
Do you offer discounts for bulk orders?
Yes, we offer volume discounts for large projects. Please contact our sales team for custom pricing based on your specific needs.
