Transcription Excellence Since 2014

Founded in 2014, JD Reporting Company has grown from a small team of dedicated transcriptionists to a leading provider of professional transcription services across Kenya and East Africa.

Our mission is to deliver accurate, timely, and confidential transcription services that help legal professionals, healthcare providers, academic researchers, and businesses transform their audio and video content into precise written documents.

  • Specialized in legal, medical, and academic transcription
  • Team of certified professional transcriptionists
  • Strict quality control and multi-level review process
  • Advanced security protocols to protect your sensitive information
  • Customized solutions to meet your specific requirements
