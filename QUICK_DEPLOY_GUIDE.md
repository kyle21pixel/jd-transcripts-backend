# 🚀 JD Reporting Company - Quick Deployment Guide

## Your App is Ready to Deploy!

### 📁 Deployment Package Location
Your deployment files are ready in: `netlify-deploy-package/`

### 🌐 Deploy to Netlify (5 Minutes)

1. **Go to Netlify**: https://www.netlify.com/
2. **Sign up/Login** to your account
3. **Click "Add new site"** → **"Deploy manually"**
4. **Drag and drop** the `netlify-deploy-package` folder
5. **Wait** for deployment to complete

### 🔧 Set Environment Variables (Important!)

After deployment, go to:
- **Site Settings** → **Environment Variables**
- Add these variables:

```
REACT_APP_API_URL = https://jd-reporting-backend-production.up.railway.app/api
REACT_APP_SOCKET_URL = https://jd-reporting-backend-production.up.railway.app
```

- Click **"Redeploy"** to apply the variables

### 🌐 Your Site URL
Your site will be available at: `https://[your-site-name].netlify.app`
